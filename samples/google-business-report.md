# Alphabet Q3 2026 Business Review

Alphabet closed Q3 2026 with **$102.4B in revenue**, up 13% year over year, and a 33% operating margin. Search and YouTube ads held steady while Google Cloud accelerated to 31% growth on AI infrastructure demand. The quarter's story is simple: AI is now lifting every segment, and capital spending is rising to keep up.

## At a glance

- Revenue: **$102.4B** (Q3 2025: $90.6B, +13%)
- Operating income: **$33.8B**, margin 33%
- Net income: **$28.1B**, EPS $2.31
- Google Cloud revenue: **$16.2B**, +31% YoY
- Capital expenditure: **$24.6B**
- Headcount: **189,000**
- Buybacks: **$17.2B**

## Revenue by segment

- Search and other: $58.1B — 56.7%
- YouTube ads: $10.9B — 10.6%
- Google Cloud: $16.2B — 15.8%

Cloud is now the second-largest growth engine after Search, and its backlog reached **$118B**, up from $92B in Q2.

## Quarterly trend

- Q1 2026: $98.3B

Four consecutive quarters of acceleration, with the steepest step in the last two.

## Segment notes

**Search and other.** Revenue grew 9% on retail and travel queries, with AI Overviews increasing session length. The team says monetization per query is stable despite the new answer format.

**YouTube.** Ads grew 11% on Shorts and connected TV. Watch time on TVs is now the largest single surface, and podcast consumption doubled year over year.

**Google Cloud.** Growth of 31% came from AI training and inference, plus data platform migrations. Operating income for the segment turned positive at $1.9B, a first for a full quarter at this scale.

**Other Bets.** Revenue of $0.6B, operating loss of $1.1B, driven by Waymo expansion and Verily restructuring.

- **Aug 14** — New TPU generation announced, targeting 2x training efficiency.
- **Sep 5** — EU DMA compliance changes shipped for search and app distribution.
- **Sep 30** — Cloud backlog crossed $100B for the first time.

## Management commentary

"Our AI investments are compounding across Search, YouTube and Cloud," said CFO Dana Whitfield. "We are funding the build-out from operating cash flow while keeping margin discipline. Q4 revenue is expected between $108B and $110B."

She added that capex will stay elevated through 2027 and that Cloud backlog conversion is the metric to watch.

## Risks

- Capital intensity: capex is now 24% of revenue and rising.
- Regulation: DMA and antitrust remedies could change default distribution.
- Ad softness: a weaker retail season would hit Search and YouTube first.
- Compute supply: TPU and GPU capacity remains the constraint on Cloud growth.

## Q4 outlook

1. Deliver $108-110B revenue with a 32-34% operating margin.
2. Convert at least $30B of Cloud backlog.
3. Hold capex near $25B while improving fleet efficiency.
4. Ship the next Gemini model to Workspace and Cloud customers.
5. Keep buybacks above $15B for the quarter.
