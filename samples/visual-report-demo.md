# Northwind Coffee Cart — Q3 2026 Review

Q3 was our strongest quarter yet: revenue reached **$86,400**, up 18% on Q2, and we sold 24,800 cups across two carts. The growth came from mobile pre-orders and a busy summer market season, but a 14% jump in bean prices squeezed gross margin from 63% to 58%. The job for Q4 is protecting margin without losing the queue-time gains that customers now expect.

## At a glance

- Revenue: **$86,400** (Q2: $73,200, +18%)
- Cups sold: **24,800**
- Average ticket: **$3.48**
- Repeat customers: **41%** of all orders
- Gross margin: **58%** (down 5 points)
- Average wait: **2.0 minutes** (down from 4.5)

## Where the money comes from

Walk-up sales still carry the cart at 52%, but mobile pre-orders grew to 31% in their first full quarter. Catering brought in 12% and wholesale beans 5%.

- Walk-up: 52%
- Mobile pre-order: 31%
- Catering: 12%
- Wholesale beans: 5%

## Monthly trend

- July: $26,100
- August: $28,900
- September: $31,400

September was the first month above $30k, helped by the library cart opening and cooler mornings.

## Best sellers

- Flat white: 6,900 cups, $23,900, 28% of revenue
- Cold brew: 5,800 cups, $19,300, 22%
- Oat latte: 4,600 cups, $17,200, 20%
- Espresso: 3,900 cups, $9,400, 11%
- Pastries: 3,600 items, $8,900, 10%
- Other drinks: 9%

## What happened this quarter

- **Jul 4** — Holiday market pop-up: 1,900 cups over one weekend, our busiest two days ever.
- **Aug 12** — Loyalty cards launched; 1,100 signups in the first two weeks.
- **Sep 2** — Bean supplier announced a 14% price increase.
- **Sep 20** — Second cart opened at the central library.

## What's working

Mobile pre-orders cut the average queue from 4.5 minutes to 2.0. Customers order from the bus and pick up without waiting. Catering inquiries doubled after we added a simple order form at the counter.

## What's not

Rain costs us real money: rainy days average 61 cups versus 138 on dry days. Monday pastry waste rose to 9% because the bake forecast still uses summer volumes. The margin drop is entirely bean cost — prices at the cart have not changed since March.

## Q4 plan

1. Renegotiate the bean contract or trial a second roaster by October 15.
2. Add a sheltered pickup window before the rainy season peaks.
3. Cap the Monday pastry bake 20% lower and track waste weekly.
4. Grow catering from 12% to 15% of revenue with two office accounts.
5. Keep the average wait under 2.5 minutes as volume grows.

## One customer said

> "I used to skip the queue on my way to work. Now I order from the bus and it's waiting when I get off." — regular at the library cart

The plan is deliberately small: protect the margin, keep service fast, and make rainy days less punishing. If those three hold, Q4 should land near $90k even without another pop-up.
